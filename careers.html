Careers

Build the thing you'd want to use

Attira is small, pre-launch, and moving quickly. If you want your work in front of real people in weeks rather than quarters, this is that.

Three roles open, all on the same team. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. There's no deadline, and we reply to everyone, including the people we don't move forward.

Content & Design Lead

  • Full-time
  • Delhi / Remote

We're looking for someone hungry, who knows the difference between work that merely functions and work that's genuinely beautiful, and can't leave that gap alone. You'll build the content system in month one and run it every day after. No brief, no team to hide behind, and no ceiling on what you own.

Attira is an AI stylist in your pocket. You build a digital wardrobe from the clothes you already own, and AIRA, our in-app stylist, tells you what to wear to class, to brunch, to the interview you've been stressing about. The app is live. Almost nobody knows it exists yet.

The role

We need a designer who can also run social. You'll build Attira's template and campaign system, then own the content that ships on it. Instagram-first, daily. Strong design experience required; hands-on social media management or content creation a must.

What you get

  • Compensation set by experience and portfolio, reviewed at six months. ESOP is under discussion for this seat.
  • Direct ownership. You are the marketing function, not a person on it. Your call on channel, tone, and calendar.
  • Founder access. You work with the founding team daily. No layers.
  • Real budget for the creator programme and campus activations, which you help allocate.
  • Attira Premium, and the merch, obviously.

Design & Social Media Associate

  • Full-time
  • Delhi / Remote

We're looking for someone hungry, with real taste. Someone who can tell a post that works from one that's actually good, and cares about the difference. You'll make a lot of things, quickly, and everything you make goes live the same week you make it. No experience needed; a portfolio and showing up matter more.

Attira is an AI stylist in your pocket. You build a digital wardrobe from the clothes you already own, and AIRA, our in-app stylist, tells you what to wear to class, to brunch, to the interview you've been stressing about. The app is live. Almost nobody knows it exists yet.

The role

We need someone who can design fast and ship daily. You'll take the template and campaign system the Content & Design Lead builds and produce against it: stories, reels, carousels, campus collateral. Canva or Figma fluency required; basic video editing a must.

What you get

  • Compensation set by portfolio and experience.
  • Room to grow. You're early enough that the marketing team gets built around you rather than above you.
  • Real published work. Everything you make goes out publicly, under a live brand, with an audience. That is a portfolio you can defend in an interview.
  • Mentorship from the Content & Design Lead and direct access to the founders.
  • Attira Premium, free, and merch.

Campus Marketing Ambassadors

  • Internship · Part-time
  • Remote + campus fieldwork
  • 12–15 hrs/week · 2 months
  • ₹1,000–₹1,500/week + incentives

We're looking for the person on campus who already knows everyone — who runs the fashion society, who's on the fest committee, who posts the fits everyone screenshots. You'll own the growth of Attira on your campus against a weekly signup target that's yours to hit. No marketing experience, follower count, or fashion degree needed.

Attira is an AI stylist in your pocket. You build a digital wardrobe from the clothes you already own, and AIRA, our in-app stylist, tells you what to wear to class, to brunch, to the interview you've been stressing about. Virtual try-on, outfit planning, and a feed of looks tuned to your actual style. We're early, the app is live, and we're building our first Campus Ambassador cohort.

What you'll do

  • Own your campus. Hostel floors, class groups, society chats, fest circles — against a weekly signup target that's yours to hit.
  • Run 1–2 activations a month. A styling booth at a fest, a wardrobe challenge, a freshers' week stall.
  • Make outfit content. Try-on reels, "what I wore this week" posts — and convert the people who engage into active users.
  • Track your own numbers in Google Analytics 4 and report weekly: which pitch lands, where people drop off, why they uninstall.

What we're looking for

  • Currently enrolled UG/PG student at a college.
  • Genuinely into fashion, styling, or personal style.
  • Well-connected on campus — you know who runs the fashion society, the fest committee, the meme page.
  • Comfortable making content. Polished isn't required; consistent is.
  • 12–15 hrs/week for 2 months.

You do not need marketing experience, a large follower count, or a fashion degree.

What you get

  • ₹1,000–₹1,500/week base stipend, performance-tiered.
  • Up to ₹2,500/month additional incentive for top performers.
  • PPO of up to ₹5 LPA for standout ambassadors as we scale.
  • Hands-on GA4 training. You'll work in a live analytics stack, build your own funnels and dashboards, and learn to read real user data — the skill most marketing graduates don't have, and the one that gets you hired.
  • Certificate of Completion and a Letter of Recommendation from the founding team.
  • Direct access to the founders — weekly calls, real ownership.
  • Attira Premium free for the programme, plus merch and a welcome kit.

Why this is different

You won't be making slide decks nobody reads. You'll own a number, run real experiments on a real campus, and see the result in a dashboard the same week.

None of these quite you? If you think you belong here anyway, write to us.
