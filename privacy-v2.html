Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy sets forth Tricore Technologies Private Limited's commitment to respecting your privacy and explains how we collect, use, share, store, and protect your Personal Data when you use Attira across India, the European Union, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

Effective date: 7 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 7 July 2026  ·  Version: 2.0

Tricore Technologies Private Limited ("Tricore", "we", "us", or "our") operates Attira ("Attira" or the "Platform"), an AI-powered fashion decision and virtual styling consumer application that helps users make clothing decisions using AI-based outfit recommendations, wardrobe analysis, and virtual try-on technology.

We Process (as defined below) Personal Data in accordance with the privacy laws applicable to you, including: (a) in India, the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025; (b) in the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"); (c) in the United Kingdom, the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018; and (d) in the United States, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA"), other US state privacy laws, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"), and state biometric-privacy laws including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act ("BIPA"), the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, and the Washington biometric statute (collectively, "Applicable Law").

Region-specific sections. This Policy applies to everyone. In addition, please read the section that applies to your region: India (Section 18), European Union & United Kingdom (Section 19), and United States (Section 20). If there is any conflict between a general provision and a region-specific provision, the region-specific provision governs for residents of that region.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all individuals whose Personal Data is collected by us through the Platform, our websites, and related services. It does not apply to information that you provide to, or that is collected by, any third party, such as social networks or app stores that you use in connection with our services. We encourage you to consult directly with such third parties about their privacy practices.

2. Definitions

2.1 "Controller" / "Data Fiduciary" means the person who, alone or jointly, determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data. For the Platform, this is Tricore.

2.2 "Processor" / "Data Processor" means any person who Processes Personal Data on behalf of a Controller.

2.3 "Personal Data" means any information about an individual who is identified or identifiable by or in relation to such data.

2.4 "Sensitive / Special Category Data" means data that receives heightened protection under Applicable Law, and for the purposes of this Policy includes biometric data and the photographs you upload of yourself.

2.5 "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether automated or not, including collection, recording, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, restriction, erasure, or destruction.

3. The Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of Personal Data:

3.1 Account Information: name, email address, account identifiers, and authentication credentials (passwords are stored only as salted hashes; if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from that provider).

3.2 Profile & Preference Information: username, bio, avatar, gender, age and/or date of birth, style preferences, favourite colours, aesthetics, style goals, and occasions.

3.3 Wardrobe Data: clothing images and garment attributes (category, colour, style, pattern, material indicators, brand), style tags, and user-added descriptions or labels.

3.4 Photographs & Biometric Data (Virtual Try-On and On-Body Photos): photographs of yourself that you upload or capture to build your profile and use the virtual try-on feature. These photographs may reveal your body shape, silhouette, and physical appearance. We derive from them body-structure/pose information ("photo profile" data) solely to position garments and generate visual outfit previews. Where this information constitutes "biometric data" or "biometric identifiers" under Applicable Law, we treat it as Sensitive / Special Category Data and Process it only with your consent. See Section 9.

3.5 Interaction & Preference Data: outfit selections, recommendation feedback, saved outfits, items rejected or ignored, virtual try-on usage, ratings/comments, AI-stylist chat messages you send, and navigation behaviour within the application.

3.6 Contextual Data: optional inputs that improve recommendations, such as occasion or event type, weather derived from your general region, seasonal context, and time-based usage patterns. We do not collect precise GPS location.

3.7 Device & Usage Analytics: collected via PostHog (hosted in the United States) — device type, operating system, app version, a randomly generated device identifier and session identifier, application performance data, usage frequency and interaction metrics, and (on our website) page views, scroll depth, clicks, and session replays with form inputs masked by default.

3.8 Diagnostics & Crash Data: collected via Sentry — error logs, stack traces, and crash reports used to fix bugs and maintain reliability.

3.9 What we do NOT collect: we do not collect precise location, contacts, microphone audio, phone numbers, payment or financial-account data, or advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), and we do not use the App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. How and When We Collect Your Personal Data

We collect the above Personal Data when you: register an account; complete onboarding and upload profile or on-body photographs; upload clothing items to build your wardrobe; use the virtual try-on feature; request or interact with AI outfit recommendations; use purchase-decision support; provide ratings, feedback, or preference signals; chat with the AI stylist; update your profile; contact us via email, chat, or forms; visit our website (via cookies and similar technologies); or engage with surveys or campaigns.

5. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use your Personal Data to:

  • Deliver and operate the Platform's core features — digital wardrobe creation, AI outfit recommendations, virtual try-on simulations, the AI stylist, and purchase-decision support;
  • Create and manage your account;
  • Build and refine your personalised style profile from your preferences, selections, and feedback;
  • Generate virtual try-on previews by combining garment images with the photographs you upload;
  • Analyse potential purchases against your wardrobe and provide compatibility and styling suggestions;
  • Respond to your queries and provide customer support;
  • Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the Platform and our AI models, including through aggregated or anonymised analytics;
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations, and prevent fraud and abuse; and
  • Any other purpose disclosed to you at the point of collection or to which you consent.

The lawful basis on which we rely for each purpose (for EU/UK users) is set out in Section 19.

6. AI, Model Training & Automated Processing

Attira uses AI to generate recommendations and try-on previews. We do not use your photographs, biometric data, or the contents of your wardrobe to train third-party foundation models, and we do not sell your data. We use aggregated or anonymised usage data to evaluate and improve our own features and prompts. Recommendations and try-on outputs are automated but are informational suggestions only and do not produce legal or similarly significant effects; where Applicable Law grants rights in respect of automated decision-making, you may exercise them as described in the region-specific sections.

7. Data Retention, Deletion & Destruction Schedule

We retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by Applicable Law, according to the following schedule:

  • Account & profile data: for the life of your account, then deleted or anonymised within 90 days of account deletion.
  • Photographs & biometric/photo-profile data: retained only while needed to provide try-on and styling features, and in any event permanently destroyed no later than the earlier of (i) the purpose being satisfied, (ii) your withdrawal of consent or deletion request, or (iii) three (3) years after your last interaction with the Platform, consistent with BIPA's retention-and-destruction requirement.
  • Wardrobe, interaction, and stylist-chat data: for the life of your account, then deleted or anonymised within 90 days of deletion.
  • Diagnostics & analytics: up to 24 months, in aggregated or pseudonymised form.

You may delete individual wardrobe items, photographs, and try-on results at any time in the app. You may delete your entire account from within the app (Account → Delete Account) or via our data-deletion request page; account deletion triggers cascading deletion of your stored images and the deletion of your authentication record. Backups are purged on their normal rotation cycle.

8. Sharing & Sub-Processors

We do not sell your Personal Data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We disclose Personal Data only to the following categories of recipients, who act as our Processors under contract and may Process it only on our instructions:

  • Google LLC / Google Cloud (incl. the Gemini API): AI model inference for virtual try-on and the AI stylist, image processing, and cloud infrastructure. Your uploaded photographs are transmitted to Google's Gemini API solely to generate your try-on previews and analyses; Google acts as our service provider/processor for this purpose.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): authentication and account management.
  • Object storage (managed cloud storage / GCS): secure storage of your images and generated previews.
  • PostHog Inc. (United States): product and website analytics, including session replay.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): crash reporting and diagnostics.
  • Apple Inc. and Google LLC (app distributors): where the app is distributed via the App Store or Google Play, certain device analytics, crash reports, and diagnostic data may be shared with the distributor and governed by its own privacy policy.

We may also disclose Personal Data to regulators, courts, or law-enforcement where required by Applicable Law, and to a successor entity in the context of a merger, acquisition, or reorganisation (subject to this Policy). An up-to-date list of sub-processors is available on request from the contact in Section 16.

9. Photographs, Biometric Data & Your Consent

Notice (Illinois BIPA, Texas CUBI, Washington and similar laws): To provide on-body profiling and virtual try-on, Attira collects and Processes photographs of you and derives body-structure/pose information from them. To the extent this constitutes a "biometric identifier" or "biometric information", we provide the following notice and obtain your written consent before collection:

  • What we collect: photographs you upload and body-structure/pose data derived from them.
  • Purpose: solely to position garments and generate virtual outfit previews and styling analysis within the Platform.
  • We do not: perform facial recognition or identity verification, sell, lease, trade, or otherwise profit from your biometric data, or disclose it except to the Processors in Section 8 that help us provide the feature.
  • Retention & destruction: as set out in Section 7 — destroyed when the purpose is satisfied, on withdrawal of consent, or within three years of your last interaction, whichever is earliest.
  • Consent: in the app, you are shown this notice and must provide affirmative consent before any photograph is captured or uploaded for try-on. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your photographs or account.

10. Children's Data

The Platform is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from children under the age of majority applicable in their jurisdiction (18 in India; 13 under US COPPA; 16 or the applicable digital-consent age in the EU/UK) without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If we learn that we have collected such data without a valid legal basis, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us Personal Data, contact us at admin@attira.org.

11. Data Security

We implement technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk — including encryption in transit, access controls, hashing of credentials, ownership checks on stored objects, and least-privilege access — to protect Personal Data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. International Transfers

We are based in India and use service providers located in the United States and elsewhere; consequently, your Personal Data may be transferred to and Processed in countries outside your own. Where we transfer Personal Data out of the EEA or the UK, we rely on an adequacy decision, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), or another lawful transfer mechanism such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, and we implement supplementary safeguards where required. A copy of the relevant safeguard is available on request.

13. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for essential functionality and for analytics (PostHog). Where required by law (including in the EU/UK), non-essential analytics and session-replay technologies are activated only after you provide consent through our cookie banner, and you may change your choices at any time.

We will verify your identity before acting and respond within the timeframe required by Applicable Law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
